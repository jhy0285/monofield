# MonoField brand

## Identity

- Product name: **MonoField**
- Wordmark: **monofield**
- Descriptor: **code / design / documents**
- Primary slogan: **One workspace, every kind of work**
- Korean slogan: **하나의 공간에서, 모든 작업을**
- Audience line: **For everyone turning ideas into outcomes.**
- Korean audience line: **아이디어를 결과로 만드는 모든 사람을 위해.**
- Category: **Local-first, open-source AI product workbench**
- Trust line: **Local-first · Open source · Model-independent**

Use `MonoField` in prose, window titles, installers, accessibility labels, and
legal text. Use lowercase `monofield` only as the visual wordmark.

Define the audience by what people accomplish, not by job titles. Do not narrow
MonoField to designers, developers, or any other fixed profession list.

## Product promise

MonoField brings code, design, and documents into one project surface. A team
can connect its repository, files, browser, database, brand context, and rules;
ask an agent to create or modify work; verify the result through diffs, builds,
browser behavior, and document review; then export or deploy it.

Do not describe the product as three separate tools. Software development and
documents/design production are starting modes inside the same project. Ask and
Docs are conversation modes that can be changed whenever the task changes.

## Visual system

- Neutral black, white, and gray form the application chrome.
- One user-selected accent may identify focus, primary actions, and selected
  state. Generated work keeps its own visual system.
- Prefer open surfaces, separators, and compact controls over decorative cards.
- The MonoField mark is a nested white and blue field inside a black rounded square.
- Product screenshots should show real diff, browser validation, and document
  review states instead of decorative gradients or abstract AI imagery.

## Vocabulary

Use Projects, Sources, Changes, Runs, Review, Deliver, and Library in user-facing
navigation and documentation. Internal compatibility identifiers such as
`@open-design/*`, `od`, `OD_*`, `open-design.json`, and existing storage keys may
remain until a versioned migration exists. They must not be presented as the
product brand.

## Attribution boundary

MonoField is an independent product. Do not display Open Design branding,
logos, slogans, or marketing copy in primary product surfaces. Required origin
and license information belongs in Settings → About → Open-source notices and
in `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in source and distributions.

Do not use Codex, OpenAI, Open Design, or another project name in MonoField's
product name, logo, or icon. Factual compatibility and comparison statements
must not imply affiliation or endorsement.
